Mobile header

  • Why it looked cream before: on mobile there was no header bar at all. The brand and the small Reglamento button sat directly on the page background inside the column. Now the same white bar with the bottom rule spans every size.
  • The pill shows only the red date block, "Asamblea General" in link blue on two lines, and a chevron. The whole pill is the link; the full label (date, Ponce, registration site) stays in the accessible name.
  • The brand gives up a little room: 36px mark and 20px wordmark on mobile, down from 44 and 22, so the pill fits beside it on a 360px screen.

Footer, variant B

  • Desktop: phone link left, the full bordered button right. Footer grows from 56 to 64px to hold the 48px button.
  • Mobile: the phone label stacks on two lines and the button uses the existing small size (44px) with "Reglamento (PDF)". One 60px row, no wrapping.
